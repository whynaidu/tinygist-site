TINYGIST

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What this app is

The TinyGist app is an internal publishing tool used only by the TinyGist team to upload and manage videos on TinyGist's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

Using Google's OAuth sign-in, the app accesses only the TinyGist channels that the TinyGist team authorises: uploading videos, editing channel and video details, managing playlists and comments, and reading channel analytics.

Data we collect from viewers

None. The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about viewers or any other users.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and used only to publish TinyGist content. They are never shared with third parties.

Google API Services

TinyGist's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: reach us through the TinyGist YouTube channel.
